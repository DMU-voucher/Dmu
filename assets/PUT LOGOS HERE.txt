Drop the two logo files in this folder and the vouchers will use them:

  dmu-logo.png              the DMU / De Montfort University Leicester logo
  food-and-drink-logo.png   the orange FOOD & DRINK logo

.jpg and .svg work too, as long as the file name matches.

Until they are here, the voucher header uses a plain text stand-in. Nothing
breaks without them, the header just will not match the existing voucher.

Transparent PNGs are best. Any size: the layout scales them by height.
