Redeeming a voucher

For staff at the two venues. Sign in once when your shift starts, then you only ever type voucher numbers.

  1. Sign in and choose your venue. Once a shift, not once a customer.
  2. Scan the QR code on the voucher, or just come back to this page.
  3. Type the nine digit number from the red box on the voucher.
  4. Green means serve it. Red means do not.
