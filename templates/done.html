Before you print

  • Print Print sheet.pdf at 100% scale, not "fit to page", or the vouchers will come out the wrong size.
  • Cut along the dashed guides. Six vouchers per A4 sheet.
  • Upload Import to redemption site.json to the redemption site's admin page, or these numbers will not be recognised when a vendor tries to redeem one.
  • Give Vendor instructions.pdf to Saints of Mokha and the street food vendor, along with the vendor password for the site.
