Accepting DMU Food & Drink vouchers

De Montfort University issues these paper vouchers to attendees at university events. They are redeemable with you. This page explains what to do when one is handed over. There is no password and nothing to sign in to.

When a customer hands you a voucher

  1. Check the voucher. The "valid until" date has not passed, the number in the red box is there and readable, and the value is the one printed in large type at the top.
  2. Write the number down or enter it, for example {{ sample.code }}. {% if cfg.vendor_record %}{{ cfg.vendor_record }}{% else %}Your DMU contact will tell you where to record it.{% endif %}
  3. Serve the order up to the value shown on the voucher.
Keep the voucher. Do not hand it back. Holding on to the paper is the only thing that stops the same voucher being used a second time, so a voucher returned to a customer can be spent again and nothing will catch it.
If the order comes to less than the voucher value, no change or cash is given, and the difference is not carried over. If it comes to more, the customer pays the difference themselves as normal.
If you cannot get online, take the voucher anyway and write the number down. Nobody should be turned away over it.

Please do not accept

  • A voucher whose "valid until" date has passed.
  • A voucher whose number is missing or damaged. Without the number there is nothing to record, and the QR code is the same on every voucher so it does not say which one this is.
  • More than one voucher against a single transaction.
  • A photocopy, or a photograph of a voucher on a phone. The paper itself has to be handed over.
