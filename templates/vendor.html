Accepting DMU Food & Drink vouchers

De Montfort University issues these paper vouchers to attendees at university events. They are redeemable with you. This page explains what to do when one is handed over.

When a customer hands you a voucher

  1. Scan the QR code with your own phone or till device. The same code is on every voucher, so bookmark the page and you can skip the scan next time. The customer should not scan it themselves.
  2. Sign in once at the start of your shift, with the password the university gave you, and choose which venue you are.
  3. Type the voucher number from the red box, for example {{ sample.code }}.
  4. Green means redeem it. Red means do not, because the voucher has already been used, has expired, or is not one of ours.
  5. Press Redeem, then serve the order up to the value shown in large type at the top of the voucher, and keep the voucher.
If the order comes to less than the voucher value, no change or cash is given, and the difference is not carried over. If it comes to more, the customer pays the difference themselves as normal.
If you cannot get online, take the voucher anyway and write the number down. Redeem it on the site once you have a signal. Do not turn a customer away over it.

Please do not accept

  • Any voucher the site does not show in green. That is the check, not the look of the paper.
  • A voucher whose number is missing or damaged. The QR code alone does not say which voucher it is, so without the number it cannot be redeemed.
  • More than one voucher against a single transaction.
